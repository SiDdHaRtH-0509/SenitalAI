# SentinelAI Demo Script (90 seconds)

## Before the demo
- [ ] Backend health check passes.
- [ ] Frontend is connected to the correct API.
- [ ] Video inference or labelled replay is ready.
- [ ] Old events are cleared or clearly identified as historical.
- [ ] Backup recording and screenshots are ready.
- [ ] IBM deployment statements are evidence-backed.

## Flow
**0–15 sec — Problem:** CCTV records footage; operators need actionable event summaries.

**15–35 sec — Event:** show the video and the configured zone. Demonstrate an observable zone-entry event. Clearly state whether inference is live or replayed.

**35–55 sec — Explanation:** show the structured event, risk score and rules/reasons. Explain that this is a prioritization heuristic, not a threat probability.

**55–75 sec — Human review:** show the recommended human verification and acknowledge the event if implemented.

**75–90 sec — IBM:** identify the exact component deployed/tested on LinuxONE and explain why it belongs there.

## Do not claim
- the system predicts criminal intent;
- a risk score is the probability of a threat;
- production readiness or guaranteed accuracy;
- IBM deployment that has not been verified.
