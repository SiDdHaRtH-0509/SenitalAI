# Contributing to SentinelAI

## Workflow
1. Confirm the official Datathon rules before writing or reusing code/assets.
2. Pull the latest `main`; create your assigned branch from it.
3. Read your Lead folder and the shared `API_CONTRACT.md`.
4. Work in your owned module; do not overwrite another lead's work.
5. Run available tests and report exact commands/results.
6. Push small commits and open a pull request. The Overall Project Lead reviews and merges.

## Branches
- Overall Project Lead: `lead/backend-integration`
- Lead 1: `lead-1/frontend`
- Lead 2: `lead-2/cv-engine`
- Lead 3: `lead-3/linuxone`
- Lead 4: `lead-4/support`

## Commit style
Use concise imperative messages, e.g. `Add event timeline empty state` or `Document s390x dependency check`.

## Safety and honesty
Never commit secrets, private footage, or unapproved assets. Clearly distinguish a replay/demo fixture from live processing. Do not claim accuracy, deployment, or production readiness without evidence.
